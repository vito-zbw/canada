title: Advance bookings and safety
summary: Several trip components sell out months ahead — corridor train cabins, Stampede weekend beds, Rockies hotels, festival passes. Book in this order and the rest of the planning is free of pressure.
lastReviewed: '2026-05-17'
items:
  - id: via-canadian-jasper-winnipeg
    text: Book the VIA Rail Canadian Jasper → Winnipeg sleeper as soon as possible
    detail: Sleeper Plus cabins for July 2026 are likely 40–60% sold by May 2026; once gone, the only fall-back is the Economy / Sleeper Plus berth.
    dueRelative: now
  - id: calgary-stampede-bed
    text: Book Calgary Stampede final-weekend accommodation (Jul 10–12) now
    detail: Hostel beds for Showdown Sunday routinely sell out by April; mid-range hotels follow shortly after.
    dueRelative: now
  - id: banff-jasper-hotels
    text: Book Banff and Jasper accommodation by April 2026 at the latest
    detail: Summer is peak season everywhere in the Rockies; Jasper's bed stock is still reduced post-2024 wildfire.
    dueRelative: by April 2026
  - id: moraine-lake-shuttle-alarm
    text: Set a calendar alarm for the Moraine Lake shuttle booking window
    detail: Opens 48 hours before each travel day at 8 a.m. MT at reservation.pc.gc.ca. Private vehicles are not allowed — the shuttle is mandatory.
    dueRelative: 2 days before each Moraine Lake day
    link: https://reservation.pc.gc.ca/
  - id: osheaga-pass
    text: Buy the Osheaga 3-day pass by April 2026
    detail: 3-day passes for Parc Jean-Drapeau sell out by June.
    dueRelative: by April 2026
  - id: stampede-rodeo-tickets
    text: Buy Calgary Stampede rodeo and concert tickets by May 2026
    detail: Final-weekend rodeo and Coca-Cola Stage concerts often sell out by May.
    dueRelative: by May 2026
  - id: feq-tickets
    text: Buy Festival d'été de Québec passes by March 2026
    detail: FEQ passes sell out by March for the July run; the on-site box office rarely has stock by arrival.
    dueRelative: by March 2026
  - id: travel-insurance
    text: Buy comprehensive travel insurance covering the first weeks in Canada
    detail: Mandatory for study permit holders before provincial / UHIP coverage kicks in. Budget CAD $150–250 for 8-week coverage from World Nomads, SafetyWing, or Manulife Travel.
    dueRelative: 2 weeks before
  - id: airfare-singapore-vancouver
    text: Book the Singapore → Vancouver flight when fares look reasonable
    detail: Indicative one-way fares are around SGD 1,200–2,000 (CAD 1,250–2,100) excluding baggage — track and book when the curve dips.
  - id: firesmoke-check
    text: Check firesmoke.ca before each flight and each Rockies hike day
    detail: Western Canadian summers can have wildfire smoke; pack N95 masks and reschedule outdoor days if AQI is bad.
    link: https://firesmoke.ca/
  - id: bear-spray-rockies
    text: Carry bear spray and hike in groups on Banff / Jasper trails
    detail: Make noise on the trail, stay alert at dawn and dusk. Solo hiking is fine if you are loud and visible; bear spray must be readily accessible, not buried in a pack.
---

These items are time-sensitive in a way the rest of the trip is not. Tick them off here once each booking is confirmed or the safety kit is packed.
