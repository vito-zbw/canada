title: Student discounts
summary: The Canada Strong Pass (Jun 19 - Sep 7) is the biggest single saving. ISIC covers some museums; the U of T TCard is the right credential once issued.
lastReviewed: '2026-05-17'
items:
  - id: canada-strong-pass-via
    text: Use VIA Rail Canada Strong Pass code CANADA1824 on Economy and Escape fares
    detail: 25% off across all routes Jun 19 - Sep 7 — does NOT apply to Sleeper Plus, Prestige, or Business
  - id: canada-strong-pass-parks
    text: Use Canada Strong Pass for free Parks Canada admission
    detail: Banff, Jasper, every national park or historic site within the same window
  - id: isic-apply
    text: Apply for an ISIC card in Singapore using the U of T acceptance letter
    detail: ~SGD 20 — covers some Canadian museums (ROM, AGO, MMFA, Vancouver Art Gallery) and is valuable for European hostels later
    dueRelative: Before flight
  - id: tcard-pickup
    text: Pick up the U of T TCard on arrival in Toronto
    detail: This is the right student ID for venues that reject ISIC (Royal BC Museum, Glenbow)
  - id: ttc-post-sec-pass
    text: Apply for the TTC Post-Secondary Pass after orientation
    detail: Available once the TCard is issued — wait until after orientation (source l62)
  - id: kananaskis-pass
    text: Buy a Kananaskis Conservation Pass for any K-Country hikes
    detail: $15/day or $90 annual; Parks Canada admission is free under the Canada Strong Pass but K-Country is a separate Alberta program (source l61, l226)
  - id: adult-fare-expectation
    text: Budget for adult transit fares everywhere except Toronto
    detail: TransLink, Calgary Transit, OC Transpo, STM all end single-ride youth fares at age 13 — expect adult fares during the trip (source l63)
