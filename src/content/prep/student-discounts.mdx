items:
  - id: canada-strong-pass-via
    text: Use VIA Rail Canada Strong Pass code CANADA1824 on Economy and Escape fares
    detail: 25% off across all routes Jun 19 - Sep 7 — does NOT apply to Sleeper Plus, Prestige, or Business
  - id: canada-strong-pass-parks
    text: Use Canada Strong Pass for free Parks Canada admission
    detail: Banff, Jasper, every national park or historic site within the same window
  - id: isic-apply
    text: Apply for an ISIC card in Singapore using the U of T acceptance letter
    detail: ~SGD 20 — covers some Canadian museums (ROM, AGO, MMFA, Vancouver Art Gallery)
    dueRelative: Before flight
  - id: tcard-pickup
    text: Pick up the U of T TCard on arrival in Toronto
    detail: This is the right student ID for venues that reject ISIC (Royal BC Museum, Glenbow)
