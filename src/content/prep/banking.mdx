items:
  - id: newcomer-account
    text: Open a newcomer chequing account online before arrival (RBC Newcomer, Scotiabank StartRight, or TD New to Canada)
    detail: Most waive monthly fees for newcomers and include a free safety deposit + a credit card
    dueRelative: 2 weeks before
  - id: wise-account
    text: Open a Wise multi-currency account and fund a CAD balance
    detail: Mid-market FX rate, tap-to-pay at every Interac terminal
    dueRelative: 1 week before
    link: https://wise.com/
  - id: sg-cards-reserved
    text: Reserve Singapore-issued DBS / UOB / OCBC cards for hotels and large refundable bookings only
    detail: 2.5-3.5% FX markup makes them costly for daily spending
  - id: cash-on-arrival
    text: Carry ~CAD 200 cash for transit or a cab on arrival
  - id: contactless-confirm
    text: Confirm tap-to-pay works on all transit cards before flying
    detail: TTC, TransLink, and STM all accept contactless credit / debit
