Four nights in the only walled city in North America north of Mexico. The Citadelle's daily Changing of the Guard anchors the in-between.

**Festivals in this window** (source l539–541):

- **Festival d'été de Québec (FEQ)** — Jul 9–19, 2026: closed before arrival; a hard miss
- **Just for Laughs Québec / Juste pour rire Québec** — Jul 22 – Aug 2: closing day overlaps with arrival evening, so any catch is OFF-JFL clubs
- **Les Fêtes de la Nouvelle-France** — typically early August historical reenactment in Old Québec; verify exact 2026 dates at [nouvellefrance.qc.ca](https://nouvellefrance.qc.ca/). If it overlaps, it's a must-do.

**Québec food specialties** (source l573–579):

- **Aux Anciens Canadiens** for traditional Québécois cuisine (maple-glazed duck, tourtière, sugar pie; $60–80/person)
- **Caribou** — fortified red-wine cocktail; available year-round in most Old Québec bars
- **Pouding chômeur** ("unemployment pudding") — Depression-era maple dessert
- **Maple everything** — *cabane à sucre* visits are mostly spring-only; the maple side of Québec lives on Île d'Orléans in summer
- **Île d'Orléans cassis** — blackcurrant liqueur tasted at Cassis Monna & Filles (Day 3 stop)
