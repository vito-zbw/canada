Four days on a Stampede rhythm, with a day off in the middle for the Glenbow Museum and Studio Bell. Hostel beds for Stampede weekend sell out by April, so the Beltline / East Village base is booked early.

**Calgary food specialties**:

- The **Caesar cocktail** — invented in Calgary in 1969 — at **Cassis Bistro** or **1886 Buffalo Cafe**
- **AAA Alberta beef** at **Charcut Roast House** or **Modern Steak**
- Brunch at **OEB Breakfast Co.** (the Hash House staple)
- **Phil and Sebastian Coffee Roasters** — the homegrown Calgary roaster
- Hipster brewery hopping in **Bridgeland** or **Inglewood** — Village Brewery and Ol' Beautiful Brewing are the anchors
