Two nights eastbound on the Park car, the dining car, and the corridor showers — Jasper to Winnipeg in roughly thirty-eight hours. Departure is in the afternoon on Mountain Time; arrival is just after breakfast on Central.

**What to expect on board**:

- The glass-domed **Park car** at the rear is first-come, first-served — claim it for the sunrise crossing into Saskatoon and again for the sunset crossing into Manitoba.
- The **dining car** runs three communal seatings per meal. Casual dress is fine but "nicer" for dinner is appreciated; expect to share a table with retired Australians and German railfans.
- **No reliable Wi-Fi or cell signal** for most of the prairie crossing — pre-download podcasts, Netflix, Spotify. Cabins have power outlets.
