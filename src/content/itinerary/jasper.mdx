Four nights at the quieter end of the Rockies. The leg ends with the 2 pm check-in for the Canadian's 3:55 pm eastbound departure.

**2026 wildfire-recovery status**: town and major attractions are open and operating (Maligne Lake, Maligne Canyon, Athabasca Falls, Miette Hot Springs, Columbia Icefield, Pyramid Lake; 75%+ of frontcountry campgrounds and all backcountry campgrounds). Still closed or limited as of late 2025 / early 2026: portions of **Cavell Road and Cavell Meadows**; parts of the **Valley of the Five Lakes trail** under realignment (some sections expected to reopen in 2026); some **Pyramid Bench** trails inside the FireSmart logging zone (Nov 2025 – Apr 2026 vegetation clearing); some hotels and restaurants still rebuilding. Check the [Parks Canada open-closed page](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/jasper/visit/ouvert-fermee-open-closed) within a week of arrival for the most current trail status.

**Jasper food & drink**:

- **The Raven Bistro** for Mediterranean fusion
- **Jasper Brewing Co.** — Canada's first national-park brewery
- **Olive Bistro** for pasta after a long hike
- **Coco's Cafe** for vegetarian breakfast
- **Bear's Paw Bakery** for to-go pastries before hikes
