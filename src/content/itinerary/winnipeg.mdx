Three nights between the train and the flight east. CMHR + Manitoba Museum + the Forks are the world-class anchors. The **Winnipeg Folk Festival** (Jul 9–12) is over by arrival and **Folklorama** (Aug 2–15) opens after departure — both narrowly miss the window. The realistic alt is the **Winnipeg Goldeyes** at Shaw Park (independent league, $20 tickets) if a home stand falls Jul 22–24.

**Winnipeg food specialties**:

- **Honey dill sauce** — the city's signature condiment, on fried chicken everywhere
- **Bison burgers** (try The Common at The Forks)
- **Perogies** at **Alycia's Restaurant** — the Ukrainian-Manitoban staple
- **Bannock** at **Feast Café Bistro** (Indigenous-owned)
- **Schmoo torte** — a Winnipeg original (cake with whipped cream and pecans)
- **Half Pints Brewing Co.** for local craft beer
